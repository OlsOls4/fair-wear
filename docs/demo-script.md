# Fair Wear — demo video script (target 2:30; trim to the rules' limit)

0:00–0:15 Hook. Text over a move-out photo: "£1,200 deposit. £640 deducted. Your evidence: 14 blurry photos." Voice: "Every year thousands of UK renters lose deposit money arguing over wear and tear with no organised evidence."

0:15–0:35 The idea. "Fair Wear turns two phone walkthroughs — move-in and move-out — into an adjudicator-ready evidence pack." Show the upload screen with two videos for a bedroom.

0:35–1:10 SAM 3.1. Split screen of both videos with tracked masks over carpet, wall, wardrobe, curtains, window. Caption: "SAM 3.1 tracks every item through each video." Then matched pairs snapping together side by side.

1:10–1:45 Muse Spark judging. Findings board: carpet — "fair wear, traffic flattening, 2 yrs of 7-yr life: tenant 0%"; wall — "damage, 6 cm gouge behind door: apportion 50% (wall 4-yr life, 2 yrs elapsed)"; curtains — "cleaning". Highlight one false-alarm the system correctly calls fair wear.

1:45–2:10 Evidence pack. Muse Image comparison card zooms in; PDF pack scrolls; draft reply to landlord's deduction list, line by line, citing TDS/DPS guidance.

2:10–2:25 Proof. "On SceneDiff, item matching hits X% precision" (fill real number); "planted-change demo flat: Y of Z changes found".

2:25–2:30 Close. "Fair Wear. Built for renters, with SAM 3.1, Muse Spark and Muse Image." Repo + live URL on screen.

Notes: all footage either CC-BY (credited in end card) or generated; label generated damage on screen ("simulated damage"). Voiceover via TTS if allowed; captions burned in.
