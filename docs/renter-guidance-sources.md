# Renter preparation guidance

Reviewed 4 November 2026. This is general preparation help, not an assessment of a specific tenancy, liability or the amount a tenant should receive.

## Why it exists

The case builder organises a response but does not submit a deposit dispute. The preparation panel keeps that distinction explicit, points to the correct jurisdiction and reminds reviewers to retain records that are not embedded automatically. It adds no required form fields or automatic external requests. Links open in a labelled new tab to avoid losing a case in progress.

## Primary sources

- [GOV.UK deposit disputes](https://www.gov.uk/tenancy-deposit-protection/disputes-and-problems): a scheme may have time limits, so contact it promptly. No universal deadline is asserted in the app.
- [GOV.UK overview](https://www.gov.uk/tenancy-deposit-protection): Scotland and Northern Ireland have separate schemes.
- [GOV.WALES deposits for tenants](https://www.gov.wales/deposits-guidance-tenants): Welsh occupation-contract and deposit guidance, including contacting the protecting scheme about disputed returns. Wales has a separate link rather than being grouped under England's guidance.
- [mygov.scot deposit returns](https://www.mygov.scot/tenant-deposits/get-deposit-back): Scottish return and dispute process and scheme checkers.
- [nidirect information for tenants](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/tenancy-deposit-scheme-information-tenants): Northern Ireland scheme and contact information.
- [mydeposits evidence preparation](https://www.mydeposits.co.uk/content-hub/evidence-for-deposit-dispute/): tenancy agreement, inventories, relevant communications and cost records; organise records chronologically and by claim, with clear labels. Quotes/estimates can support costs; a completed invoice is not made a prerequisite.
- [TDS relevant evidence FAQ](https://custodial.tenancydepositscheme.com/tools-and-guides/faqs/tenants/what-evidence-do-i-need-to-submit/): submit evidence relevant to the disputed issue rather than unrelated material.
- [DPS fair wear and betterment](https://www.depositprotection.com/learning-centre/disputes/in-disputes-be-fair): assessment depends on evidence and context, and must allow for fair wear and tear without betterment. No fixed useful life or deduction percentage is sourced from this page.

Guidance copy and links are shared between the page and exported HTML through lib/renter-guidance.ts. The response draft itself is unchanged. Source links are informational and require an internet connection, but the bundle content remains available offline.
